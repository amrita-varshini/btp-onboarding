# Tell Us What You Think

Your feedback helps SAP improve the Joule Work Desktop experience. This is a collaborative step — share your experience, report issues, and engage with the SAP team.

---

## Share Your Feedback

Please share:

- What worked well and what was challenging
- Features you would like to see prioritized
- Use cases you are exploring with JWD
- Any documentation gaps or unclear steps

---

## How to Provide Feedback

### Via Your SAP Contact

Reach out directly to your **SAP** contact or Customer Success Partner with:
- A summary of your onboarding experience
- Any blockers or issues encountered
- Feature requests or improvement suggestions

### Via the DC Mission Collaboration Board

If your DC Mission has a collaboration channel or board:

1. Navigate to your DC Mission board
2. Add a comment or note on the **Tell us what you think** card
3. Rate the mission and share your top takeaway

---

## Bug Reports and Support

If you encounter a technical issue during setup:

| Issue Type | Contact |
|---|---|
| Provisioning or SAP4ME issues | Your SAP support contact |
| IAS / Identity Services issues | SAP Identity Services support |
| JWD client issues | SAP support |
